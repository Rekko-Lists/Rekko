# 📜 Rekko Rules

> These guidelines exist to keep the community healthy, safe, and enjoyable for everyone.

---

## 🚨 Quick Summary

| Allowed                   | Prohibited           |
| ------------------------- | -------------------- |
| ✅ Sharing opinions       | ❌ Harassment        |
| ✅ Recommending anime     | ❌ Discrimination    |
| ✅ Respectful discussions | ❌ Unmarked spoilers |
| ✅ Posting fan art        | ❌ Spam              |

---

## 1️⃣ Respect and Community Conduct

### 🚫 Harassment or Bullying

**Severity:** Suspension

#### Examples

- Personal insults
- Threats
- Ongoing harassment
- Coordinated harassment campaigns

### 🚫 Discrimination and Hate Speech

**Severity:** Permanent Ban

#### Examples

- Racism
- Xenophobia
- Homophobia
- Hate messages

### ⚠️ Toxic Behavior

**Severity:** Warning

#### Examples

- Constant provocation
- Repeated insults
- Destructive behavior

---

## 2️⃣ Appropriate Content

### 🔞 Sexual Content

**Severity:** Permanent Ban

Explicit sexual content is not allowed.

### 📢 Unmarked Spoilers

**Severity:** Warning

Always use spoiler tags when discussing major plot points.

### 🩸 Graphic Violence

**Severity:** Suspension

Extreme gore or real-life violent content is not allowed.

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement System

| Level         | Consequence    |
| ------------- | -------------- |
| 🟡 Warning    | Private notice |
| 🟠 Suspension | 3–30 days      |
| 🔴 Ban        | Permanent      |

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if I make a mistake?

Minor first-time violations will usually result in a warning.

### Can I appeal?

Yes. You have 30 days to submit an appeal.

# 🌸 About Rekko

> The anime social network where you can discover, share, and connect with fellow fans.

---

## ✨ Welcome to Rekko

Rekko is much more than just an anime list.

Here, you can:

* 📺 Organize everything you watch
* ⭐ Rate your favorite series
* 💬 Share your opinions with the community
* 🔍 Discover new anime
* 🎮 Play the daily Animedle
* 🤝 Meet people who share your interests

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

> We believe anime is more than entertainment.

It is a way to connect with others, discover unique stories, and express who we are.

Our goal is to build a community that is:

| Value         | Description                      |
| ------------- | -------------------------------- |
| ❤️ Inclusive  | All fans are welcome             |
| 🛡️ Safe      | Active moderation and protection |
| 🌟 Authentic  | Real opinions from real users    |
| 🚀 Innovative | New ways to discover anime       |
